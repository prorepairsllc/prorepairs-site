Commercial Facilities Management

Scaled operations expertise applied to commercial FM environments — vendor accountability and predictable performance across multi-site portfolios.

Integration of external contractors into a unified operational framework. Real-time visibility into work quality, completion timelines, and standards compliance — tied to portfolio outcomes, not individual site decisions.

Multi-site lease obligations tracked at portfolio level. CAM tracking, deferred-maintenance backlog management, capital vs. lease-covered repair triangulation, and proactive documentation to minimize disputes.

Consolidated visibility into maintenance spend and vendor pricing across sites. Data-driven identification of spend leaks and performance gaps, preventing reactive, emergency-mode repairs.

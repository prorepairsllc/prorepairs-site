Operations at Scale

ProRepairs built a multi-state property maintenance and HVAC service platform, scaling to 127,000+ annual transactions across Michigan and Florida. The operation combines distributed field teams, integrated vendor networks, and custom workflow systems—delivering predictable service standards and operational performance across a complex, multi-site portfolio.

Commercial Facilities Management

ProRepairs brings scaled operations expertise to commercial FM environments, managing multi-site portfolios with vendor accountability and predictable operational performance:

Integration of external contractors into unified operational framework. Real-time visibility across all vendor activity—work quality, completion timelines, compliance with standards. Performance metrics tied to portfolio-level outcomes, not individual site decisions. Eliminates fragmentation across disparate vendor platforms.

Multi-site lease obligations tracked and coordinated at portfolio level. CAM tracking, deferred maintenance backlog management, capital vs. lease-covered repair triangulation. Proactive documentation and vendor coordination to minimize lease disputes and cost exposure.

Move from ad-hoc, site-level decision-making to scalable, repeatable operational processes. Custom intake systems that route work appropriately, reducing unnecessary escalations. Clear ownership and decision frameworks across departments—maintenance, housekeeping, guest services, owner relations.

Consolidated visibility into maintenance spend, vendor pricing, and operational efficiency across multiple sites. Data-driven identification of spend leaks, unnecessary duplicate services, and performance gaps. Proactive backlog management to prevent reactive, emergency-mode repairs.

Company Evolution

ProRepairs' trajectory reflects disciplined operational scaling and strategic adaptation:

2017–2019: Foundation
Single-person diagnostics and property maintenance operation. Built customer relationships on accuracy and trust—not high-volume churn.
2020–2021: Strategic Pivot
Residential HVAC and appliance demand surged. Transitioned to full-service appliance repair and replacement. Integrated property maintenance into comprehensive portfolio. Scaled vendor network to meet demand.
2022–2024: Scale & Optimization
Built custom intake/dispatch platform. Standardized SOPs across departments. Grew to 127K+ annual transactions. Established repeatable operations across Michigan and Florida.
2025: Acquisition
Acquired by private equity investors. Platform, vendor network, and operational systems integrated into broader property management portfolio.
